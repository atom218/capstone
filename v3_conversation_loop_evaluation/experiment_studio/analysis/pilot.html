Subscription C3 detailed: Claude’s largest specificity gain, +0.57, and a joint-highest product-insight score. C4 detailed: Claude’s largest user-consequence gain, +0.43. C1 detailed is another balanced candidate, with higher means on all three product-quality criteria.

Basis for the comparison

One baseline and eight grounded combinations per product: four journeys × brief or detailed, each with seven answers and the same full biography. The two short-biography runs are excluded. Each condition has one saved interview; these are descriptive findings, not independent replications or significance tests. Brief and detailed configurations change both behavioural instructions and journey delivery.

Saved judge identifiers: claude-opus-5-5-fast and gpt-6.1-sol. The same 126 answers are scored by both judges. Conversation-consistency means exclude Q1, so they use six applicable answers.

The message to take away

In these interviews, grounding helps surface concrete risks, boundaries and product needs that a baseline answer can leave unstated.

The anecdotes show what the extra value looks like: refund eligibility, another month’s bill, a temporary pause, or a clearer boundary around personal information.

Encouraging evidence

We have specific combinations that outperform their baselines, and actual answer examples that make the gains understandable.

A focused next step

Repeat the most promising combinations across more interviews and personas to see whether these gains hold.

Brief context for interpreting the findings

There are 18 matched conditions: one baseline and eight grounded combinations per product (four journeys × brief or detailed). Each condition has one saved interview. These are descriptive results, not proof of a general or causal effect. Brief and detailed combinations change both behavioural instructions and journey delivery. Scores are on a 0–3 scale. The anecdotes are selected same-question comparisons, not a count of how often grounding helps. A point absent from the matching baseline answer may appear elsewhere in the baseline interview.
